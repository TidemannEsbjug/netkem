About NetKem

Expertise from Mobil Oil & ExxonMobil — applied to aquaculture

NetKem AS was founded in 2001 by Øistein Antonsen, who worked with these products for many years at Mobil Oil and later ExxonMobil. The expertise remains, and new products are continuously under development.

The company collaborates extensively with leading aquaculture research institutes, and supplies both chemical products and technical equipment such as net washing machines.

Core areas

Four product areas for modern aquaculture

We develop solutions for the entire operating cycle — from impregnation before deployment to wash and disinfection between cycles.

Net impregnation

Effective protection against fouling, UV and drying out. Gentle on the net and approved for all common net materials.

Coating

The Netpolish series creates a smooth, durable film that shortens cleaning time both on land and at sea.

Net cleaning & wash

Water-based wash that preserves net strength and quality, with strong action on algae and old impregnation.

Technical equipment

Patented Gigant washing machines in sizes from 9 m³ to 70 m³, with adjustable programs.
